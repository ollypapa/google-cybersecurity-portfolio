# Professional Statement

## Step One: My Strengths

### 1. Technical Troubleshooting and Problem Solving

I have experience identifying technical issues, investigating their causes, and working toward practical solutions. My experience in technical support has taught me to stay calm when dealing with difficult issues and to approach problems step by step.

I am also developing my ability to analyze problems from a security perspective. I want to understand not only how to fix a problem, but also why it happened, what risks it creates, and how similar problems can be prevented.

### 2. Communication and Interpersonal Skills

My work in technical support requires me to communicate with customers, users, developers, product teams, and other stakeholders. I have learned how to explain technical issues in simple terms and work with different people to resolve problems.

I believe good communication is important in cybersecurity because security teams need to work with both technical and non-technical people.

### 3. Endurance and Willingness to Learn

I am committed to continuously developing my technical knowledge. Moving from technical support into cybersecurity requires me to learn new concepts, tools, and security practices.

I am willing to put in the time and effort needed to grow in the field and develop practical cybersecurity skills.

---

## Step Two: My Values

### 1. Protecting People and Information

I value protecting people, systems, and sensitive information from threats, fraud, and unauthorized access. I believe cybersecurity is not only about protecting technology, but also about protecting the people who depend on that technology.

### 2. Following Laws, Policies, and Ethical Standards

I believe security professionals have a responsibility to follow laws, policies, and ethical standards. I also value fair and equitable access to secure technology.

Security should protect users while allowing them to safely access the services and information they need.

---

## Step Three: Clarifying Questions

### 1. What Most Interests Me About Cybersecurity?

I am interested in cybersecurity because I want to help protect people and organizations from threat actors, security risks, and digital fraud.

I see cybersecurity as a way of protecting lives and property, similar to the role of military or security personnel, but with the focus on protecting systems, data, and people in the digital world.

My interest also comes from my experience working with technology and healthcare systems. Healthcare systems handle sensitive patient information, so protecting these systems is very important.

I want to understand how cyberattacks happen, how security risks can be identified, and how security professionals can prevent, detect, and respond to threats.

### 2. Who Is the Audience for My Professional Statement?

My main audience is cybersecurity recruiters, security teams, and organizations looking for people with technical support experience and developing cybersecurity skills.

I am particularly interested in organizations where cybersecurity, data protection, and technology are important, including the healthcare sector in Nigeria.

My experience supporting healthcare technology has given me an understanding of the importance of protecting systems that handle sensitive patient information.

I want my portfolio to show employers that I am serious about building a career in cybersecurity and that I am actively developing practical skills through the Google Cybersecurity Certificate and hands-on projects.

### 3. How Can My Strengths, Values, and Interest in Cybersecurity Support Organizations?

My technical troubleshooting experience can help me investigate security issues, identify problems, and understand how technical systems behave when something goes wrong.

My communication skills can help me work with users, technical teams, and other stakeholders when dealing with security incidents.

My commitment to protecting people and information gives me a strong reason to take cybersecurity seriously. I want to help organizations reduce security risks, protect sensitive information, respond to incidents, and create safer systems for their users.

As I continue learning cybersecurity, I want to combine my existing technical support experience with new skills in areas such as:

- Security Operations
- Incident Response
- Network Security
- Linux
- SQL
- Vulnerability Management
- Security Monitoring

My goal is to grow into a cybersecurity professional who can understand both technical security problems and the people affected by them.
